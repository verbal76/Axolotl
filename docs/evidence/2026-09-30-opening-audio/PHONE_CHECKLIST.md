# Phone checklist: 00037-opening-audio

- [ ] At launch, the loading screen sounds like a household aquarium (soft water movement, a distant
      bubbler), not static or hiss.
- [ ] It fades in gently; volume is comfortable; no crackle or click.
- [ ] No obvious short repeating loop over a minute or two of listening.
- [ ] When the title music starts, the bubbling eases under it smoothly; no cut, jump or doubled sound.
- [ ] New Run / Continue into play: the same bed continues, no second bubbler layered on top.
- [ ] Sound slider at 0 silences it; mute works; volumes are remembered after restarting the app.
