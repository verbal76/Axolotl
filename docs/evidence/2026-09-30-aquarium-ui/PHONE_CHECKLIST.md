# Phone checklist: aquarium, UI, bedroom, fish and swim package

For the b22 phone once the OTA is active (Settings → About / Diagnostics shows the new build).
Anything that looks wrong: a screenshot and a line of what you expected is enough.

## Title
1. The title shows MOTE, the run line, Continue / New Run / Gill's colours / Aquarium, and a gear
   top right. Nothing overlaps; no grey or black boxes.
2. Tap the gear: Settings opens with Show run timer, Reduced HUD, Haptics, **Invert swim
   up/down**, Gill's colours, Music, Sound and About / Diagnostics at the bottom.
3. **Back** returns to the title. The run time on the title has not moved; nothing started.
4. Buttons visibly darken or shift while pressed.
5. About a second after the title appears there may be one short hitch (the bedroom being built);
   note it if it is longer than about a second.

## Gill's colours
6. Title → Gill's colours: controls on the left, Gill on the right on a studio stage, all of him
   visible.
7. Drag on Gill: he turns; a flick keeps him turning briefly. Dragging him never scrolls the
   controls.
8. Scroll the controls down to Reset / Done: Gill stays where he is.
9. Tap a preset and move a slider: he changes at once. Moving a slider never turns him.
10. Done, quit the app, reopen: the colours you chose are kept.

## Aquarium
11. Title → Aquarium: a warm, lived-in dusk bedroom round the tank (TV with a game on, a messy
    dresser, a bed with a bear, bookshelves, a window at dusk).
12. The tank: on a dirty save the glass is algae at the edges and filmed in the middle, and you can
    still make out moss balls and fish; on a cleaner save it's clear and planted.
13. **Live Tank**: fish are clearly visible, including the three big silver bala sharks.
14. **Swim**: exactly two controls, a stick on the left and a round SWIM button on the right.
    - Pull the stick down: his nose goes up. Push up: nose down. Left/right turns him.
    - Hold SWIM: he swims where his nose points. Let go: he glides to a stop.
    - Settings → Invert swim up/down reverses only up and down.
15. Back out to play: Gill is exactly where he was, the timer carries on, nothing else changed.

## Also useful
16. Frame rate in the bedroom, Live Tank and Swim feels as smooth as before.
17. Diagnostics after a session: send the screenshot (boot health, active build).
