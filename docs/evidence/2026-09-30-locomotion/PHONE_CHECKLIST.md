# Phone checklist: Gill's locomotion, the pillar columns and the Treasure Hunt pickup

For the b22 phone once the OTA is active (Settings → About / Diagnostics shows the new build).
A screenshot and a line of what you expected is plenty for anything that looks wrong.

## Gill's body
1. Turn sharply from a standstill: his head turns first and his body curves after it in a C, then
   straightens. The controls should feel exactly as quick as before.
2. Run in a curve: his tail follows the path his head took.
3. Walk up and down a slope or over a hill: his front tips onto the slope first and his body lies
   along it, not level and floating.
4. Jump: his head leads the arc.
5. In Swim Mode (Aquarium → Swim): his body bends through turns and when diving or climbing.

## Traction
6. Walk into a short, sharp lip (about ankle to knee height for him): he pulls himself over it
   instead of stopping dead.
7. Walk into a steep mossy face or a rock wall: he can't climb it, and doesn't stick to it. He
   slides back down; pushing again and again never inches him up.
8. Ravine walls: still no way up except the designed routes.
9. Jump, burst, lunge, falls and combat feel unchanged.

## Pillars and bubble columns (Open Issue #2)
10. World 7 (Hollow Grotto), the Shaft: a bubble column now rises right beside the pillar's cap,
    with a soft cyan glow at its base. Ride it up and drift onto the cap to reach the Regeneration
    Mote on top.
11. The same in World 1's Fern Grove shelves and World 2's High Hollows shelf.

## Treasure Hunt (when you reach 100%)
12. A lunge through a tall object (the rubber duck) always collects it.

## Also useful
13. The spot where you first noticed Gill facing into a mossy slope and getting stuck: whether it
    now feels right.
14. Diagnostics after a session: a screenshot (boot health, active build).
